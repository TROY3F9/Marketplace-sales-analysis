# Marketplace Sales Analysis

This project was designed to help marketplace managers understand sales performance and customer behavior, addressing the challenge of making sense of large volumes of sales data. By transforming raw transactions into actionable insights, it enables faster, data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards and analytics.

##  Key Features & Insights

Managers often struggle to track sales performance, identify trends, and understand customer behavior, making it difficult to optimize inventory, prioritize products, and plan effective marketing strategies. This project addresses these challenges by:

* Revealing sales trends over time (daily, weekly, and monthly) to identify seasonality and performance patterns.
* Identifying top-performing products, categories, and marketplaces to support inventory and marketing decisions.
* Analyzing customer purchasing behavior to improve customer engagement strategies.
* Calculating key business KPIs such as revenue, profit, and growth rate.
* Transforming raw sales data into an interactive Power BI dashboard for real-time business insights.
